No. Sound baths are beginner-friendly. You only need to arrive, breathe and allow the soundscape to support rest.

Can Nishi come to my location?

Yes. Sessions may be arranged in homes, studios, offices, wellness spaces or event venues in Charlotte and nearby areas.

Is sound healing medical treatment?
