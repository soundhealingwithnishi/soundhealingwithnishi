Charlotte sound bath FAQ

Is this a public event or private session?

Nishi offers private, couples, group, corporate, retreat and event sessions. Some public events may also be listed separately when available.

Do I need experience with meditation?

No. A sound bath is beginner-friendly. You can simply lie down or sit comfortably and receive the sound.

Can Nishi come to my location?

Yes, sessions can be requested for homes, studios, wellness spaces, offices and events in Charlotte and nearby areas.

Is sound healing medical treatment?

No. Sound healing is a wellness and meditation experience. It is not medical treatment and does not replace professional healthcare.
