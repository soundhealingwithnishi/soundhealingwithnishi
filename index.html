About Nishi

Charlotte's first
vibrational sound healer

Nishi Bhansali is Charlotte, NC's first vibrational sound healer — a certified sound healing practitioner who works with hand-picked Himalayan bowls, crystal bowls, and 18+ instruments to bring the ancient wisdom of sound into modern spaces, helping people quiet the noise and come home to themselves.

Her sessions create a deeply immersive soundscape where your body can let go, your nervous system can soften, and your mind finds stillness. Whether one-on-one or in a group, each experience is held with care, intention, and deep presence.

Based in Charlotte, NC — offering individual, group, and corporate sound meditation sessions, and available to travel worldwide for retreats and events.
