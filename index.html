About Nishi

A global journey into sound, softness & healing

Nishi’s path began with curiosity, movement, and a love for discovering the world. She has traveled through places like Dubai, Singapore, Sri Lanka, Switzerland, Europe, and Sydney, Australia, where she completed her studies and spent six years building a career in the corporate tech world.

After returning to India, her heart pulled her toward wellness, energy, and deeper inner connection. That passion led her to explore sound healing across India and become certified in Rishikesh, a place known for yoga, meditation, and spiritual learning.

After marriage, Nishi moved to Charlotte, North Carolina, carrying with her the same calm, grounding energy she discovered on her own journey. Through Sound Healing with Nishi, she now shares immersive vibrational sound experiences with Himalayan bowls, crystal bowls, tuning fork, gong, and 18+ instruments.
